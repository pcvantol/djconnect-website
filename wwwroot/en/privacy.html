Your listening. Your privacy.

This website uses no tracking cookies. Download and install redirects may count aggregate clicks by day, destination and source; they do not store IP addresses, user agents or personal identifiers.

DJConnect and your data

Your Home Assistant manages your connection and music accounts. App credentials stay in protected app storage. Depending on your configured voice and AI providers, requests and audio may be processed by those providers through Home Assistant.

Optional personalisation

Music DNA is opt-in in Home Assistant and can be cleared there. Ask DJ also works without Music DNA. Clients do not build a separate persistent listening profile.

Notifications and support

Optional Apple notifications use a central relay for wake and sync hints. They do not include account tokens or full Ask DJ conversations. If you email support, we process your message to help you.

Privacy questions

Contact support@djconnect.dev for privacy questions or deletion requests.
