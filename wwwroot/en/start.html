DJCONNECT

A good evening starts here.

DJCONNECT works with Home Assistant. Your Apple app guides you through connecting, choosing your DJ and starting your first session.

1. Your home for music

You need a running Home Assistant installation with DJConnect, a configured music account and a playback device.

Set up DJConnect in Home Assistant↗

2. Open DJConnect

Open the DJConnect Apple app and follow its onboarding wizard. Connect to your Home Assistant, choose your profile and let your DJ begin.
